Protect your collection

In Settings → Backup & Export, choose Save JSON Backup and keep the file somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive. A JSON backup includes your books, covers, loan information and earned milestones. Choose Restore JSON Backup to merge it into ShelfTrack; existing matching books are skipped rather than overwritten. CSV is useful for spreadsheets, but cannot restore your library.

Deleting ShelfTrack removes its local library. Reinstalling does not automatically recover it. Export before uninstalling. Do not send a full backup to support unless you intend to share its contents.

Adding books

Choose Scan, Enter ISBN, Search Online or Enter Manually. Review the details before adding. Online sources can have incomplete or incorrect information; you can correct the title, author and cover. Local browsing and manual entry work without an internet connection.

If camera access is unavailable, use manual ISBN entry or manual details. You can change camera permission in iPhone Settings → ShelfTrack.

Loans and reminders

Choose Lent to someone or Borrowed from someone and type a name or optionally choose a contact. Due dates and notification permission are optional. These are reminders for you, not messages sent to your contact. Mark Returned clears the active loan and its scheduled reminders.

Sharing

Share milestone on your shelf or Share book from Book Details. Review the image before choosing a destination. Available apps and actions depend on the device. Sharing does not include private notes or loan details; cover photos can still contain personal information.

Report a problem

For the private beta, use TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback. Include device model, iOS version, ShelfTrack version/build, route used, network condition, expected result and actual result. Screenshots help; remove private names and notes first.

For support by email, use the same information. Never send passwords, payment details or your Apple Account credentials.
