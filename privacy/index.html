Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

ShelfTrack is published by Teofil Pop. Contact: iteofilpop@icloud.com.

This policy describes the ShelfTrack iPhone/iPad app. It does not replace the privacy policies of Apple, book-information providers or services you choose for sharing and file storage.

Your library

Your books, covers, notes, ratings, categories, loan details and earned milestones are stored locally on your device. ShelfTrack has no account system, advertising or third-party analytics SDK. It does not currently provide automatic cloud sync or automatic recovery after reinstalling.

Online book information

Online search, ISBN lookup and metadata refresh use Open Library. Missing authors and covers may be checked with Google Books using the ISBN. These requests send the relevant ISBN, search title/author, or book-record identifiers to the provider. Image requests contact the cover provider. Providers receive normal network information such as your IP address and may keep logs under their own policies. Your personal notes, ratings and loan contact names are not included in these requests. Local browsing and manual entry work offline.

Camera, Photos and Contacts

The camera is optional and is used to scan barcodes or photograph a cover. Photos selection provides the chosen cover image. Contact selection stores only the selected display name, not the address book, email address, phone number or contact identifier. You can enter a person's name manually. Cover adjustment runs on your device; selected images are not uploaded by ShelfTrack.

Loan reminders

Optional reminders are local device notifications, not messages sent to the person on the loan. They can show a book title and a person's name, including on the lock screen depending on your notification settings. Returning or deleting a book cancels its scheduled reminders. You can change notification permission in iPhone Settings.

Sharing

Sharing is optional. Preview your card before choosing a destination in the system share sheet. A book card includes its cover, title and author; a milestone card includes its award and current collection count. Personal notes, ratings, ISBN and loan details are excluded. A cover photo may itself contain personal information, so review it before sharing. Temporary sharing files are removed after completion or cancellation; abandoned files are removed on the next launch. The selected destination handles the shared image under its own policies.

Backup and deletion

JSON backups include all saved book fields, cover data, loan names and earned milestones. CSV exports include book metadata, personal notes, ratings and loan names. You choose where exported files are saved, for example Files or iCloud Drive. Anyone who can access those files may see their contents. Delete a book to remove it from the current library; earned milestones remain. Deleting the app removes its local library. Copies in exports, shared images or device backups remain until you remove them through the relevant storage service. Save a JSON backup before uninstalling; CSV cannot restore your library.

Getting help

Email iteofilpop@icloud.com for support. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and what happened. Do not include contact names, private notes or a full library backup unless you intend to share that information. Support email contains whatever you send and is handled through the publisher's email service. TestFlight feedback is handled by Apple and may include screenshots, device details and crash information.

Retention and privacy requests

Library information remains on your device until you edit/delete it or remove the app. Earned milestones remain after individual book deletion. Exported files and copies shared elsewhere are controlled by you and the services receiving them. You can delete local data without contacting the publisher.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve the request, maintain necessary support records or meet legal obligations. Email the publisher to request access, correction or deletion of support information you supplied. Information held independently by Apple or other providers must be managed with those providers.
